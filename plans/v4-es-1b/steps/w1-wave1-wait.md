# Wait: wave W1 is collected and reconciled

Wait step. No human action. The driver checks the gate and does not start an agent. The gate passes when the submitter (step s01) has collected every ticket of wave W1, including the one retry of errored and expired requests, and every request has a result or a line in a `failed` file.

Wave W1: the full Jury matrix, the editorial triage, the humor panel and the back-translation. This is the bulk of the review.

## If it waits too long

- Under 24 hours after the last submission: wait. The completion window of both APIs is 24 hours.
- Over 24 hours: run `python3 tools/batch_submitter.py status` in the operator shell. Check that the timer runs and that the keys are set there. If a ticket is not submitted, its estimate is probably over the cap: the status line says so. Raise the cap in `plans/v4-es-1b/records/f02-operator-budget.md` (a commit) or remove the ticket.
- Failed requests: the next import step counts them. It fails if more than 2 % of the requests of the wave failed. Then Jim decides: resubmit as a new ticket, or accept the gap and record it in the import record.
